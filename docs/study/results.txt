Protocol section 6 excludes "anchors whose span is non-unique at capture".
The implementation checked uniqueness only WITHIN a document, not across the
corpus, so 112 anchors were already AMBIGUOUS at the first horizon. Those can
never show STALE, so in principle they dilute the headline.

  AS RUN (pre-registered)          n=1012  stale=95  S(365d)=0.901  9.9% @ 1y
  degenerate-at-capture excluded   n= 900  stale=83  S(365d)=0.902  9.8% @ 1y

why it is reported rather than applied. The AS RUN figure is the headline.

Of 114 anchors ever AMBIGUOUS, 112 were ambiguous from the first observation
and only 2 became ambiguous later. Cross-document sentence duplication in this
corpus is a starting condition, not a decay mode.
